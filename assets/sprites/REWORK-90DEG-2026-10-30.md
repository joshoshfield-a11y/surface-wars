# Surface Wars — 90° top-down sprite rework spec (prepared 2026-10-30)

Why: the user reiterated on 2026-10-30 ~05:21 PDT that the new RTS is
strict 90° top-down only — no angled / orthographic / diagonal views.
The sprite batch completed 2026-10-30 ~05:19 PDT is otherwise clean
(124 distinct sprites / 248 files, 5 contact sheets), but its own
reports flag the classes below as failing that bar. They are held for
rework BEFORE any wiring into the prototype, which still uses canvas
placeholder silhouettes. Nothing here is wired, committed, or published.

### C. Flagged for the same pass, not blockers for wiring
- structures/aircraft_hangar — reads mainly as a runway strip with only
  a small hangar end-block.
- units/field_repair_mech — least instantly legible (spider-like legged
  mech from above).
- terrain tiles generally — grid-sliced tiles may not tile seamlessly
  edge-to-edge (2% inset trim); edge cleanup / seam work needed before
  repeating use, plus transition tiles are missing entirely.
- -512 files do not preserve relative class scale (Scout = Rampart at
  512px) — in-game scaling must come from game data, not the files.

## Explicitly NOT in this pass
- No animation frames, damage states, or wreck variants (never generated).
- No FX/projectiles, UI icons, Salvage Clans / Directorate skins.
- No wiring into ~/workspace/surface-wars/game/ — that stays a separate
  step after this rework passes a fresh contact-sheet QC.
- No copying of Rusted Warfare or any other game's assets; regenerated
  sprites stay original work in the spirit of the user's RTS screenshot.

## Acceptance (per the sprite-batch-QC skill)
- Fresh contact sheet per reworked category, viewed at game size.
- Every reworked class: strict 90° overhead, no diagonal barrel/dish,
  no foreshortening; red variants team-readable.
- Counts + report updated in SPRITES-REPORT.md; flawed classes get one
  simplified regeneration pass, then stop and report — do not loop.
