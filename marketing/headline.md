# Surface Wars — headline (Loop 1 + Wave-5 re-check, staged draft, NOT published)

## The headline

**Top-down RTS battles where every wreck is worth scrap.**

## Why this headline

- Repeatable in 5 seconds: one promise, no jargon, no numbers a stranger has to remember.
- Every word is demonstrable in the current game: strict 90° top-down view, real-time
  battles, destroyed units left on the field as wrecks, wrecks worth 40% of their build
  cost, salvageable by any unit, value decaying to zero over 120 seconds.
- It does not claim anything the game cannot show: no multiplayer, no campaign, no
  performance claim, no comparison to any other game.

## Wave-5 re-check (v1.7 content) — headline UNCHANGED, and why

- The headline still holds word for word: the 8 new mechs leave wrecks under the
  same 40%-over-120-seconds rule, so the wreck count behind the promise grows
  from 49 unit types to 57 with no rule change.
- Nothing in v1.7 competes with the headline for the top line: Tutorial II,
  twin-stick control, the hero, mastery, and the Premium Store are all second
  lines (they are in `listing-draft.md`), and none of them changes what a
  wreck is worth.
- No new claim was smuggled in: the headline still promises no real-money
  purchase, no price, and no release date.

## What the headline does NOT promise

- It does not promise a finished game. The current build is a playable slice:
  Iron Eden vs Xandria, skirmish on three maps, two tutorials, landscape-first mobile play.
- It does not promise a price, a release date, or a platform beyond what is listed
  in `listing-draft.md`. Those are open decisions for the owner (see that file).
- It does not promise real-money purchases: the Premium Store runs on earned
  Salvage Credits only, and real-money purchases are not live.
