# Surface Wars — sharp advantage (Loop 1, staged, NOT published)

## The sentence

**In Surface Wars, destroyed units stay on the battlefield as wrecks worth
40% of their build cost for 120 seconds, and any unit can salvage them — so
battles fund the next battle instead of just ending it.**

## Why it qualifies

- It is one specific mechanism, not a generic claim like "fun" or "deep".
- It is demonstrable in the current build: wrecks are real records, their
  value decays on a fixed timer, and salvage pays the decayed value.
- A generic top-down RTS where destroyed units simply vanish could not
  honestly make this claim about its own game, because the mechanism is not
  in it. This sentence describes what Surface Wars actually does, not what
  its competitors do not do.
- It does not name, copy, or compare itself to any other game.

## Wave-5 note — sentence UNCHANGED

- The mechanism is identical in v1.7: wrecks are still real records, still
  40% of build cost, still decaying on the same 120-second timer, still
  salvageable by any unit. The only change is coverage: 57 unit types
  now leave wrecks instead of 49, because the 8 new mechs got wreck art.
  No word of the sentence needed to change, and no new advantage was
  claimed on top of it (the store, mastery, and hero are separate
  lines in `listing-draft.md`, not folded into this sentence).
