# Surface Wars — audience-specific openings (Loop 1, staged, NOT published)

Facts are identical across all three openings. Only the entry point differs.
No opening claims affiliation with any other game, and none copies another
game's names, stats, or art.

## (a) RTS players

**A strict top-down RTS with a scrap economy: build, fight across a single
bridge, and salvage the wrecks you make.**

Why it differs: it opens on the systems an RTS player checks first — economy,
map control, and what happens to units after they die.

## (b) Mobile gamers

**A full RTS built landscape-first for your phone, with two tutorials
that teach you by playing — core moves, then twin-stick control and the hero.**

Why it differs: it opens on the device and the learning curve, which is what
a phone player has to trust before they trust the strategy layer.
Wave-5 reason for the change: the old line said "a 7-step tutorial", which
is now incomplete — Tutorial II is the second tutorial, and a phone player
who only reads this opening should know both exist before trusting the
learning curve. The "teaches you by playing" promise is unchanged and
still true of both.

## (c) Players of large-scale top-down strategy games

**Massed units, mechs, and artillery on an 80×50 battlefield, where terrain
and a single bridge crossing decide the fight.**

Why it differs: it opens on scale and terrain, which is what a player of
large battles listens for first.

## Shared facts behind all three (for checking, not for pasting everywhere)

- Strict 90° top-down view.
- Iron Eden vs Xandria; scrap economy starting at 500 scrap.
- Wrecks worth 40% of build cost, decaying over 120 seconds, salvageable.
- First Map is 80×50 with a river and one bridge crossing.
- 7-step interactive tutorial + Tutorial II (twin-stick and hero);
  landscape-first mobile play.
- 8 new mechs (Iron Eden: Bastion, Gatekeeper, Deadeye, Mortarjack;
  Xandria: Needlewing, Longsight, Javelin, Raptor).
- Premium Store on Salvage Credits only; real-money purchases not live.
- No multiplayer, no campaign, no fog of war, no research (see listing-draft.md).

## Wave-5 update notes — what was left alone, and why

- Opening (a) RTS players: LEFT ALONE. It opens on economy, map control,
  and wrecks — all still true, and the bridge line still describes the
  First Map. Adding mechs or the store here would blunt the systems
  opening it exists for.
- Opening (c) large-scale players: LEFT ALONE. Scale and terrain are
  still the hook, and the 80×50 battlefield line is still true on all
  three maps. The new mechs are a roster detail, not a scale claim.
- Only opening (b) changed, because only it counted the tutorials.
